    The build is the one-off part. The surprise for most founders is the running bill: GPS apps don't just sit on a server, they talk to a mapping platform all day, and those platforms charge per use. Google Maps Platform's pricing shows the model — every map load, route calculation and location check is a small charge, and they add up with usage. Light months cost little; heavy tracking doesn't. Our guide to running costs covers the other monthly bills too.

    How your app uses mapsWhat the monthly mapping bill looks like
    Light lookupsSmall — users check a location now and then
    Route-heavyGrows with use — every route calculated is billed
    Always-on trackingThe big one — constant location pings, constantly billed

    The moment founders miss is tracking. Live tracking updates every few seconds, and each update is a billable event. Ten vehicles tracking all day costs far more than a thousand people checking a map once — design for how often your app talks to the map, not just how many users you have.

    That's why apps that move things — deliveries, rides, trades — feel this bill first. A food delivery app's cost breakdown is a good example: the location tracking that makes it work is also its quiet monthly expense. Decide early how live your app truly needs to be, and the numbers stay friendly.
