FAQ

Questions we hear every week

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

How much does a website cost?

A content-managed business website usually starts from $3,000. A website with a full online store usually starts from $6,000. Every site is designed for your business, so we give you a clear quote after a free consultation.

How long does it take to build a website or app?

A simple website takes about 3 weeks. A site with e-commerce takes 4 to 8 weeks. Larger platforms and apps with integrations take around 3 to 6 months. Gathering content and feedback quickly keeps projects on schedule.

How do I know my app idea is worth building?

Good ideas solve a real problem people face often. Search Google and the app stores to see what already exists, define who your target audience is, and think about how often they would use it. We help you test all of this in a free strategy session.

Can you turn my existing website into an online store?

Yes. We add an e-commerce system that fits how you sell, with clear product pages and a simple checkout, because good interface design directly increases sales.

How can an app make money?

An app is a small business. Common models are paid downloads, subscriptions, in-app purchases and advertising. We help you choose the model that fits your audience before we build.

Can you work on software someone else built?

Yes. We review your existing code, fix issues, improve performance and add new features without starting from scratch.
