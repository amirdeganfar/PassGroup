What moves the average cost to make an app

Four things do most of the moving:

  • Screens — every distinct view needs design, coding and testing.
  • Platforms — one (iOS or Android) costs less than both.
  • Integrations — connections to outside services like payments or SMS reminders — each needs setup and ongoing care.
  • Who builds it — freelancers cost less than studios; established agencies cost more than both.

All of them are optional to include in version one — that's the lever.
