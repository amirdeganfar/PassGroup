The surprise for most Perth founders isn't the price — it's the wait. How much do app developers cost in Perth? The same broad bands as the eastern states: a founder-budget build with a local studio lands in the low-to-mid five figures, and larger agency projects run beyond that. Perth's dev market is smaller, though, and the good studios book out — so check availability before you compare a single quote. Hourly, expect the usual spread: freelancers near the bottom of the rates Australian app developers charge, established studios near the top. What's genuinely different in Perth is pool size. Fewer studios means fewer quotes to compare, which puts more weight on checking portfolios hard — two live apps you can download today beats any pitch deck.

It also changes how you run the project. With a smaller local bench, remote-first arrangements are worth accepting rather than fighting: video demos every week, one shared board, payments on milestones. The best Perth projects we've seen run exactly that way, with the city mattering far less than the cadence of working software.

How much do app developers cost in Perth vs the east coast?

The bands barely move between cities: Melbourne app development costs and Sydney's sit at the top of the same range, and Perth tracks alongside rather than below. What Perth does lead on is growth — the ABS had Greater Perth growing fastest of any capital at 2.4% a year — and a bigger city means steady demand for the same senior developers. That's why the wait matters. When we price work for Western Australian founders, the first honest conversation is calendar, not budget: the right team available now beats a cheaper one available next quarter.

Start conversations before your brief is perfect. Perth studios book out in advance, and a week of early emails is the cheapest way to shorten a wait of months.
