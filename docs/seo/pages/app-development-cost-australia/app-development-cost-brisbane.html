How much do app developers cost in Brisbane vs the national bands?

Use the national bands and you'll quote-proof any Brisbane proposal. A lean but professional first app sits around $20,000–$50,000; add accounts, payments and a server side and $30,000–$60,000 is realistic. Agencies bill toward the top of the hourly rates Australian app developers charge; freelancers sit near the bottom with thinner safety nets. A quote well under those bands hasn't costed testing — or it's scoped to shrink.

The city itself is part of the story. Brisbane is one of the fastest-growing capitals in the country — the ABS puts Greater Brisbane at about 2.8 million, growing over 2% a year — and demand for senior developers grows with it. In our experience, Brisbane founders who start conversations early get the better prices; those who wait for the "perfect" brief watch the calendar do the quoting for them.

Brisbane, Melbourne or Sydney for your build?

For most projects, the city matters less than the team. Rates in Melbourne's app development market and Sydney's sit at the top of the same national band, with deeper benches of specialists. A good Brisbane studio matches them on quality at similar money — the difference is pool depth, not talent. Remote-first projects are normal now, so shortlist locally and interstate and pick on proof.

Do Brisbane developers cost less than Sydney ones?

Not much, for like-for-like scope. Brisbane rates track the national bands and land within noise of Sydney's on most projects. Treat a big gap as a question to ask, not a bargain.
