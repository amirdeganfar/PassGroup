When a restaurant actually needs an app

A subscription ordering or booking tool is free to try and tops out around $50 a month — for a single venue, that usually wins. A custom build earns its keep when your regulars order weekly, you want your own brand and customer list, and you'd rather pay once than rent bookings forever. The government's guide to selling online groups food delivery apps with marketplaces for a reason: their fees run higher than your own channel — exactly the money a well-judged app claws back.

Start with website + tools (most venues)

  • Ordering or booking subscriptions — free to about $50 a month
  • Found on Google, nothing to download
  • Build an app later, from real order data

Build the custom app (when it fits)

  • Loyal regular crowd ordering every week
  • Your brand, your customer list, your rules
  • Menu, ordering and bookings in one place you own
