The fee isn't the interesting part — the name on the account is. How much does an Apple developer account cost? US$99 per year, billed annually, for the membership that lets you publish on the App Store. Free Apple accounts exist, but they can't publish. Pay the US$99 in your own or your company's name — never your developer's — and the app, its listing and every future update stay under your control. Individual or organisation? Choosing your enrolment

Apple enrols you as an individual or an organisation. Individuals sign up under their own legal name — the quick path for sole traders. Organisations enrol under the company name, which is what you want when the app belongs to a business: Apple verifies the company details, so expect an identity check and a little paperwork rather than an instant approval. Either way the fee is the same US$99 a year — and the account, not your developer's logins, should belong to whoever owns the app.

US$99 a year buys the membership; the renewal is the part people forget. A lapsed payment can pull a live app off the store — diarise it from day one.

Set the account up in your name

The calls we get usually start the same way: a founder built an app with a freelancer, the freelancer holds the developer account, and now contact has gone quiet. The app, its reviews and its update pipeline sit inside someone else's login. Transfers are possible but slow, and starting over means losing the app's history. Getting the setup right costs nothing:

  • Register with your own Apple ID — or a company one you control
  • Use a business email address that stays with the business, not a personal inbox
  • Give your developer access through App Store Connect instead of handing over the account
  • Store the sign-in and recovery details with your other business credentials

Account ownership is the same conversation as who owns the app code — they should have the same answer: you.

  • Day 0 — enrol as individual or organisation, pay US$99
  • During the build — add your developer as a team member; they work, you own
  • Renewal — Apple bills the card on file; a failed payment lapses the membership
  • Every year after — same US$99, same account, your app still live
