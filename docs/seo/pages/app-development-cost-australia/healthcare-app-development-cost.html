Two apps can look almost identical: one books gym classes, one books doctor appointments. Yet the healthcare one will cost more. The honest answer to how much does it cost to develop a healthcare app depends less on the screens and more on the data you touch. A simple wellness or booking app starts in the five figures; anything holding patient data needs specialist care and a bigger budget. The app development cost guide for Australia gives you the wider picture.

Health features don't cost extra because they're hard to draw. They cost extra because health data is private — and privacy takes real work.

How much does it cost to develop a healthcare app with patient data?

Patient data changes everything. Health records are some of the most protected information in Australia, and the rules around them add design, security and testing work that ordinary apps skip — the OAIC's privacy guide for organisations is the plain-English starting point. Expect extra budget for secure logins, encrypted storage (that's data scrambled so only the right people can read it) and careful review with the health professionals who'll actually use it.

5 figureswhere simple wellness and booking apps start — patient-data apps budget well above this

Not every health idea needs that level of protection, though. A booking app for a physio clinic sits much closer to the cost of a booking app than to a hospital system. A step counter that never stores identifiable health data sits closer to a fitness app build. The line is the data itself: the moment you hold someone's medical history, you're in the protected zone.

Keeps costs sane

  • Pick one clear use case and build that well
  • Map exactly what data you'll hold before quoting
  • Loop in the clinicians who'll use it early

Blows the budget

  • Storing patient data "just in case" you need it
  • Assuming any app developer knows health privacy
  • Leaving security testing to the last week

In my studio, health app quotes almost always turn on one early question: what data do you truly need? I've watched a project shrink its budget meaningfully just by deciding not to store medical records at all in version one. That decision is free — and it's the cheapest one you'll make.
